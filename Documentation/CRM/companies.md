# Companies Guide

Companies are business records in CRM.

This page helps keep company information in one place.

## How To Open Companies

1. Click `Marketing`.
2. Click `CRM`.
3. Click `Companies`.

## What You Can Do

1. add company
2. search company
3. filter company status
4. edit company
5. view company
6. delete company

## What The Page Shows

1. company name
2. website
3. industry
4. company size
5. city
6. country
7. status

## Common Company Status Examples

1. Prospect
2. Active Vendor
3. Partner
4. Customer
5. Inactive
6. Blacklisted

## How To Add Company

1. Click `Add Company`.
2. Fill company details.
3. Save the record.

## Good Company Usage

1. one company should not be repeated many times
2. keep website correct
3. keep location correct
4. keep status updated
5. link the right contacts and deals to the company
