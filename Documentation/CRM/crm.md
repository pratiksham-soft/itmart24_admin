# CRM Full Guide

CRM is the place where sales and follow-up work happens.

It helps the team:

1. collect leads
2. talk to leads
3. qualify leads
4. turn leads into real opportunities
5. manage follow-up work
6. send campaigns
7. track progress

3. `Contacts`
4. `Companies`

6. `Tasks & Follow-ups`
7. `Activities`
8. `Email Campaigns`
9. `Segments`
10. `Reports`
11. `CRM Settings`

## How CRM Works In Easy Order

1. A new person or business comes in as a `Lead`.
2. The team checks the lead details.
3. The team calls, emails, or follows up.
4. The lead status moves step by step.
5. If the lead is good, the team converts the lead.
6. After conversion, contact, company, and deal records can be used.
7. The deal moves through the sales pipeline.
8. The deal ends as `Won` or `Lost`.

## Best Daily Work Order

1. Open CRM Dashboard.
2. Check new leads.
3. Update lead status.
4. Create follow-up tasks.
5. Convert ready leads.
6. Move deals in pipeline.
7. Review activities.
8. Send campaigns if needed.
9. Check reports.

## Simple Tip

Good CRM work means one thing:

Do not leave leads stuck without the next step.
