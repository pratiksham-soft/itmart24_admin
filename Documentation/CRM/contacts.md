# Contacts Guide

Contacts are people records inside CRM.

Usually a contact is linked to a company and used for real follow-up work.

## How To Open Contacts

1. Click `Marketing`.
2. Click `CRM`.
3. Click `Contacts`.

## What You Can Do

1. add contact
2. search contact
3. filter by lifecycle
4. edit contact
5. view contact
6. delete contact

## What The Page Shows

Each row can show:

1. contact name
2. email
3. company
4. job title
5. contact type
6. lifecycle stage
7. next follow-up

## Lifecycle Stage Examples

The CRM supports stages like:

1. Subscriber
2. Lead
3. Marketing Qualified
4. Sales Qualified
5. Opportunity
6. Customer
7. Partner
8. Inactive

## How To Add Contact

1. Click `Add Contact`.
2. Fill the form.
3. Save the contact.

## How To Use Contacts Properly

1. keep one contact per real person
2. link the contact to the right company
3. update lifecycle stage when the relationship changes
4. keep follow-up timing clean

## When Contacts Are Used

Contacts are useful after the lead becomes more real and structured.

Very often a lead is converted first, then contact work becomes more important.
