# Email Campaigns Guide

This page is for bulk CRM outreach.

It sends emails one by one with delay.

## How To Open Email Campaigns

1. Click `Marketing`.
2. Click `CRM`.
3. Click `Email Campaigns`.

## What You Can Do

1. create campaign
2. edit draft
3. duplicate campaign
4. start sending
5. send test email
6. watch progress
7. cancel sending
8. delete campaign

## What You Need First

1. an active sender account from Email Manager
2. CRM leads with valid email addresses
3. subject line
4. email message

## Campaign Status List

1. `Draft`
2. `Sending`
3. `Completed`
4. `Failed`
5. `Cancelled`

## How To Create Campaign

1. Click `Create Campaign`.
2. Fill campaign name.
3. choose sender account
4. choose delay in seconds
5. choose body mode
6. write subject
7. write body
8. choose recipients
9. preview email
10. save draft or start sending

## Body Mode

1. `HTML` = styled email
2. `Text` = simple email

## How To Select Recipients

1. search CRM leads
2. use recipient filters
3. tick needed leads
4. or use `Select All Filtered`
5. check selected list

## Personal Tokens

1. `{{firstName}}`
2. `{{lastName}}`
3. `{{companyName}}`
4. `{{jobTitle}}`
5. `{{website}}`
6. `{{email}}`

## How To Save Draft

1. For a new campaign click `Create Draft`.
2. For an old draft click `Save Draft`.

## How To Start Sending

1. Use `Create & Start Sending`
2. or `Save Draft & Start Sending`
3. or open a draft and click `Start Sending`
4. confirm the sender, count, delay, and subject

## How To Send Test Email

1. open campaign details with `View`
2. type your test email
3. click `Send Test`

## What You Can See In View

1. total recipients
2. sent count
3. failed count
4. pending count
5. progress bar
6. email preview
7. recipient delivery table

## Recipient Status Meaning

1. pending
2. sending
3. sent
4. failed
5. skipped

## How To Cancel Sending

1. find the sending campaign
2. click `Cancel`

## Good Rule

Always preview and test before sending a large campaign.
