# Deals And Pipeline Guide

Deals are active opportunities.

This page is where real opportunity tracking happens.

## How To Open Deals

1. Click `Marketing`.
2. Click `CRM`.
3. Click `Deals / Pipeline`.

## Two Views

The page has:

1. `Pipeline View`
2. `Table View`

You can switch between them.

## Deal Stage List

1. `New`
2. `Qualified`
3. `Demo Scheduled`
4. `Proposal Sent`
5. `Negotiation`
6. `Won`
7. `Lost`

## How Deal Flow Works

1. A lead is converted.
2. A deal is created.
3. The deal moves stage by stage.
4. The deal ends in `Won` or `Lost`.

## How To Add Deal

1. Click `Add Deal`.
2. Fill title and deal details.
3. Save the deal.

## What A Deal Usually Has

1. title
2. source
3. stage
4. value
5. currency
6. probability
7. expected close date

## How To Move Deal In Pipeline View

1. Open `Pipeline View`.
2. Find the deal card.
3. Move it to the new stage.
4. The system updates the stage.

## How To Work In Table View

1. Click `Table View`.
2. Read deal rows in a list.
3. Edit or delete if needed.

## Stage Meaning

### New

The opportunity has started.

### Qualified

The opportunity looks real and useful.

### Demo Scheduled

A demo or important meeting is planned.

### Proposal Sent

The team already sent business terms or pricing.

### Negotiation

Final discussion is happening.

### Won

The deal was successful.

### Lost

The deal did not close.

## Easy Rule

Every active deal should always be in the correct stage.
