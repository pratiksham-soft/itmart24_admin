## Lead Journey Properly Explained

This is the simple full lead journey.

### Stage 1: New

Meaning:

The lead just came in.

What to do:

1. check name, email, phone, and company
2. check source
3. check if it is real or duplicate
4. decide who should handle it

### Stage 2: Contacted

Meaning:

The team already tried to talk to the lead.

What to do:

1. call or email the lead
2. write a note
3. create a follow-up task
4. wait for reply

### Stage 3: Qualified

Meaning:

The lead looks useful and real.

What to do:

1. confirm the need
2. confirm budget or business interest if possible
3. confirm product or service fit
4. keep follow-up active

### Stage 4: Demo Scheduled

Meaning:

The lead is ready for a demo, meeting, or deeper talk.

What to do:

1. create a task for the meeting
2. fix the date and time
3. add all notes after the meeting

### Stage 5: Proposal Sent

Meaning:

The team sent pricing, proposal, listing offer, or business plan.

What to do:

1. mark that the proposal was sent
2. create follow-up task
3. wait for feedback

### Stage 6: Negotiation

Meaning:

The lead is discussing terms, pricing, or final details.

What to do:

1. record all updates
2. keep the next follow-up date ready
3. answer questions fast

### Stage 7: Converted

Meaning:

The lead is no longer only a lead.

The team is ready to turn it into active CRM records.

What happens:

1. use `Convert Lead`
2. the system can create related records
3. a contact can be created
4. a company can be created
5. a deal can also be created

After this:

1. continue work in `Contacts`
2. continue work in `Companies`
3. continue work in `Deals / Pipeline`

### Stage 8: Lost

Meaning:

The lead is not moving forward now.

Possible reasons:

1. no reply
2. wrong fit
3. wrong budget
4. not interested
5. selected another option

What to do:

1. write the reason in notes
2. stop active chasing
3. keep history for future reference

## Easy Lead Journey In One Line

`New -> Contacted -> Qualified -> Demo Scheduled -> Proposal Sent -> Negotiation -> Converted`

Or:

`New -> Contacted -> Qualified -> Lost`

Or:

`New -> Lost`

## How To Add A New Lead

1. Click `Add Lead`.
2. Fill the lead form.
3. Choose lead type.
4. Choose lead source.
5. Choose lead status.
6. Choose priority.
7. Add next follow-up date if needed.
8. Save the lead.

## How To Import Leads

1. Click `Import Leads`.
2. Download the sample CSV.
3. Fill your CSV file.
4. Upload the CSV.
5. Preview the result.
6. Click `Import Leads`.
7. Read the summary:
8. created
9. updated
10. skipped
11. failed

## How To Add A Note

1. Open the lead.
2. Click `Add Note`.
3. Write the note.
4. Save it.

## How To Add Follow-up Task

1. Open the lead.
2. Click `Add Follow-up Task`.
3. The system creates a follow-up task.
4. Check the task later in `Tasks & Follow-ups`.

## How To Convert A Lead

1. Open the lead.
2. Make sure the lead is really ready.
3. Click `Convert Lead`.
4. The system converts it.
5. Continue work in contacts, companies, and deals.

## When To Convert A Lead

Convert when:

1. the lead is real
2. the lead is interested
3. the team needs formal CRM records
4. the deal is ready to be tracked

## When To Mark A Lead Lost

Mark lost when:

1. the lead is not interested
2. the lead is fake or wrong
3. the budget does not match
4. the business fit is not correct
5. repeated follow-up got no real result

## Good Simple Lead Rules

1. every lead should have a status
2. every active lead should have a next step
3. every important talk should be written in notes
4. do not convert too early
5. do not leave a dead lead active forever
