# Products Guide

This section is for product checking and product editing.

Products are split into different status pages.

## Product Pages

1. `Pending Products`
2. `Claimed Products`
3. `Active Products`
4. `Rejected Products`
5. `On Hold Products`
6. `Delete Products`

## How To Open Products

1. Log in to ITMart24 Admin.
2. In the left menu, click `Products`.
3. Click the product status page you need.

## What You Can Do On Product Pages

- search products
- filter by status
- open product details
- edit product fields
- move products to another status

## How To Search Products

1. Open a product page.
2. Use the search box.
3. Type product name, vendor, category, plan, or ID.
4. Look at the filtered list.

## How To Change Product Status

1. Open the product status page.
2. Find the product row.
3. Use the status dropdown in the row if shown.
4. Pick the new status.
5. Wait for the update to finish.

## How To Open Full Product Details

1. Find the product.
2. Click `View`.
3. The product detail window will open.

## What You Can Check Inside Product Details

1. Product overview
2. Snapshot
3. Classification
4. Feature data
5. Media gallery
6. External links
7. Pricing summary
8. Plan breakdown
9. Ownership and verification
10. Link verification
11. Shopify status
12. Raw record data

## How To Edit A Product

1. Open product details.
2. Click `Edit`.
3. Change the fields you need.
4. Save the changes.

## Things You May Edit

- product name
- vendor ID
- category
- sub category
- tags
- Shopify URL
- pricing
- plan rows
- claimed status
- verification fields
- link fields

## Easy Safe Workflow

1. Search the product.
2. Open `View`.
3. Read all important details first.
4. Click `Edit` only after checking the record.
5. Save small, correct changes.

## Simple Tip

If you are not sure, do not change many fields at one time.

Change one part, save, and check again.
