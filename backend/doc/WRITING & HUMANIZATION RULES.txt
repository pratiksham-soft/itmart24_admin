CONTENT WRITING & HUMANIZATION RULES — MANDATORY

Whenever you create, update, import, rewrite, or publish any customer-facing content, write it like a professional human content writer, not like an AI assistant, developer, database importer, or documentation writer.

The final content must sound natural, polished, premium, commercial, reader-friendly, and suitable for a real SaaS/software marketplace website. It must never feel AI-generated, robotic, placeholder-like, over-explained, or copied from internal data.

Write as if the content is final website copy written by an experienced SaaS, software, and digital product content writer.

STRICTLY DO NOT USE THESE PHRASES OR SIMILAR VARIATIONS:
- as per supplied data
- as per provided data
- based on supplied data
- based on provided information
- according to the data
- according to the supplied information
- from the provided details
- from the supplied content
- as a marketplace
- in this marketplace
- marketplace context
- Shopify pricing
- Shopify product
- Shopify data
- Shopify listing
- this product data
- product metadata
- metafields
- Firestore
- collection handle
- product ID
- source file
- imported data
- vendor-provided data
- generated description
- AI-generated
- this page provides
- this section explains
- users can find
- users can compare
- the platform offers
- the product is listed
- the vendor has provided
- official data says

DO NOT mention internal systems, backend logic, Shopify, metafields, Firestore, APIs, product IDs, collection handles, import scripts, source files, database fields, admin panel fields, or any technical/internal source of the content in customer-facing text.

DO NOT write content that sounds like:
- a database record explanation
- an AI-generated summary
- a product import note
- a developer comment
- a documentation paragraph
- a placeholder description
- a robotic SEO paragraph

TONE REQUIREMENTS:
- Professional
- Natural
- Human
- Confident
- Helpful
- Clear
- Premium
- Commercial but not pushy
- SEO-friendly without keyword stuffing
- Written for buyers, vendors, founders, or business users depending on context

STYLE REQUIREMENTS:
- Use normal human sentence flow.
- Keep paragraphs short and readable.
- Avoid repeating the same phrase structure.
- Avoid overusing the product name.
- Avoid generic AI phrases such as “robust solution,” “seamless experience,” “empower businesses,” “cutting-edge,” “unlock potential,” and “revolutionize” unless they genuinely fit.
- Avoid repetitive phrases like “users can,” “businesses can,” “this tool helps,” again and again.
- Prefer specific benefits, practical use cases, buyer intent, decision-making value, and business outcomes.
- Make every sentence sound useful to a real visitor.
- Write with the quality expected from a premium SaaS marketplace, software directory, comparison website, or startup landing page.

CONTENT STRUCTURE RULES:
For product descriptions:
1. Start with a natural buyer-focused sentence explaining what the product helps with.
2. Mention practical benefits and common use cases.
3. Explain who it is useful for.
4. Close with a helpful buying, comparison, or evaluation-focused sentence.
5. Do not mention where the information came from.

For category descriptions:
1. Explain what the category covers in natural language.
2. Mention common product types or use cases.
3. Help buyers understand how to compare options.
4. Keep it useful, search-friendly, and non-generic.

For comparison pages:
1. Write like a neutral expert comparison writer.
2. Help readers understand the difference between products.
3. Focus on use cases, pricing considerations, features, support, scalability, and decision factors.
4. Avoid fake certainty if exact data is unavailable.
5. Do not say “as per supplied data” or “based on provided data.”

For vendor-facing content:
1. Sound premium, founder-friendly, and opportunity-driven.
2. Avoid cheap discount language.
3. Emphasize trust, visibility, early advantage, growth potential, and long-term value.
4. Keep the tone professional and credible.

FINAL HUMANIZATION PASS — REQUIRED BEFORE SAVING:
Before saving, publishing, or returning the final content, review every generated line and rewrite anything that sounds:
- AI-generated
- robotic
- repetitive
- generic
- overly formal
- unnatural
- source-referencing
- database-like
- placeholder-like
- keyword-stuffed

Final output must not contain even one sentence that sounds like it was written by AI or copied from supplied data.

QUALITY CHECK BEFORE COMPLETION:
Confirm internally that:
1. No forbidden phrases are present.
2. No internal systems or source references are mentioned.
3. The content sounds like professional human website copy.
4. The tone is natural, polished, and commercially useful.
5. The text is suitable for public display on ITMart24 or a similar premium SaaS/software marketplace.
6. The content is SEO-friendly but not spammy.
7. The wording is unique, varied, and not repetitive.

If any generated content fails these rules, rewrite it automatically before final output.
